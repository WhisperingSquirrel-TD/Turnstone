OWNER'S REHEARSAL, ROUND 2 (owner-approved 14 Oct; amends SPEC §3 and §10). Do these as part of the rehearsal bug fixes (step 2 of RESUME ORDER), under TDD.

A. Moving on
1. In solo rehearsal the player is the organiser and gets the organiser's controls, including "Move on" on every step. Never show "The organiser will move the game forward" to the organiser.
2. A timed step ends as soon as every player who must act has acted; bots act immediately. Secret steps still respect their minimum private duration, so timing reveals nothing. Debate has nothing to complete: it ends at its timer or when the organiser taps "Move on".
3. With several real players plus bots, a step waits only for the real players.

B. Voting without holding
4. The banishment vote is an ordinary screen: no shield, no hold. Tap a name, confirm, done. Do not show the player's role on it; the screen is identical for everyone.
5. Choice lists never include the player themselves or anyone who is out.
6. Private choices (night pick and Faithful decoys, recruitment, Notes and Traitor chat): keep "Shield your screen" and "I'm shielded", then show the choice screen without holding. It hides on Done, when the app goes to the background, and after 30 seconds without a tap; one tap brings it back. Layout and timing stay identical for every role. Choices fit inside the viewport from 320x568 up; long lists scroll within the screen.
7. Hold-to-reveal stays only for seeing your role at the deal.
8. Shields: secret shield holders stay selectable at night (marking them would reveal who holds one). A publicly won shield shows "Shielded" beside that name at night, still selectable. Shields never affect the banishment vote.

C. Check
9. On wider screens the top-right icon looks like a loading spinner that never stops; on a narrow phone the Stackstone mark sits there instead. Find out which is intended and fix.

The full-rehearsal end-to-end test must reach the results screen using "Move on" and early step endings, with no screen left waiting on a timer the player cannot affect.
