Concept A

The shared table

Seven equal stones and one slightly larger gold seat complete a circle around open space: every player has a place, with one clear brand accent.

Trade-off: the most literal social signal, with the gold seat improving recognition; the remaining stones may still merge at favicon size.

The turnstone token
