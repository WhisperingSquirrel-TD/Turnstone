Identity study · not a production mark

Three ways to gather.

A small visual exploration of the Turnstone icon and wordmark. The palette, role neutrality and existing serif wordmark direction stay fixed; the metaphor and small-size construction change.

Large lockup and 32 px icon tested on both Charcoal and Candle. Miniature one-colour proof included.

Concept A

The shared table

Eight equal stones make a complete circle around open space: every player has a place, and no seat carries a tell.

Trade-off: the most literal social signal, but the repeated small stones can merge at a glance.

Concept B

The standing-stone circle

Four matching monoliths gather around a low round table, giving the mark a more atmospheric game-night presence.

Trade-off: the clearest ritual mood, with more enclosed detail and less breathing room at favicon scale.

Concept C

The turnstone token

Three equal stepping stones pivot around a quiet centre, translating the existing stacked-stone language into a compact game token.

Trade-off: the sharpest 32 px silhouette and closest brand link, but the gathering metaphor is less literal.

Recommendation: take Concept A forward. Its clear ring of equal seats honours the owner’s stone-circle idea directly, while the open centre keeps the silhouette less crowded than the standing-stone version at small sizes. Concept C is the fallback if the 32 px icon proves to be the dominant touchpoint.
