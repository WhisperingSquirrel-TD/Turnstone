---
name: Night theme precedence
description: Owner-required night darkness and the scope of saved Display choices.
---

Night screens, including private choices and waiting views, must always use
the dark theme. Saved Match my phone, Light and Dark choices govern day and
entry/setup screens only. Entering night must not change the saved choice.

**Why:** The owner explicitly required this on 25 October 2026 after a saved
Light choice left the night screen in the paper theme.

**How to apply:** Drive the override only from the public night phase,
identically for every role. Check Light → night → day and preserve the
original preference. The morning DAWN screen remains a day screen.
