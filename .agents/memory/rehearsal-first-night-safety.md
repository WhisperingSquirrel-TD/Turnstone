description: The completion signal for rehearsal's human-protection window.
---

Rehearsal bot targeting must keep the human seat ineligible until the first completed Dawn is recorded. A cycle number or murder-resolution record is not, by itself, proof that the human completed a full day and night.

**Why:** The full server-backed rehearsal exposed that bot eligibility must be tied to the game's actual Dawn completion, so the human cannot be banished or taken during their protected first cycle.

**How to apply:** Use the public game log's completed Dawn event as the safety boundary; test that all first-cycle bot vote and night targets exclude the human, then verify normal targeting can resume afterward.
